Accessibility statement

Built for every visitor

I want every student, colleague and visitor to be able to use this site, whatever device, browser or assistive technology they rely on. If something here gets in your way, please tell me.

Report a barrier

01 Standard

The standard this site follows

This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. Under the U.S. Department of Justice's ADA Title II rule, public universities must make their web content meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA, and WCAG 2.2 includes all of those requirements.

02 Features

What's built in

  • Keyboard accessEverything works with a keyboard. A "Skip to content" link comes first on every page, and focused items get a clear outline.
  • Screen readersPages use real headings, landmarks and lists. Images have text descriptions, buttons have names, and filters and copy actions are announced.
  • Readable contrastText meets at least a 4.5:1 contrast ratio in both the dark and light themes.
  • Light and dark themesUse the sun or moon button in the menu bar. The site also follows your device's setting.
  • Motion you controlThe pause button in the menu bar stops every animation, and the site turns animations off automatically if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Zoom and reflowText can be enlarged to 200%, and pages reflow to a single column on screens as narrow as 320 pixels.
  • Accessible documentsThe CV, research statement and teaching portfolio PDFs are tagged, with a title and language set, so screen readers can navigate them.
  • Captioned videoAny video I post will have accurate captions and a transcript.
  • No time limitsNothing on the site expires or requires a fast response.
  • Clear linksLinks that open a new tab tell screen-reader users before they're followed.

03 Testing

How it's checked

Every page is tested with axe-core against the WCAG 2.2 A and AA rules in both color themes, and color pairs are checked against the contrast formula directly. Headings, focus order, zoom and narrow-screen layouts are also reviewed by hand. This statement was last reviewed on October 29, 2026.

04 Limitations

Known limitations

  • Other websites. Links to Google Scholar, arXiv, publishers and other sites lead to pages I don't control, and their accessibility may vary.
  • Publisher PDFs. Some papers are hosted by publishers as PDFs that may not be fully accessible. I'm glad to send an accessible version of any paper I've written.
